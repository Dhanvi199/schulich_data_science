Connect Beyond Barriers

We empower adolescent girls with autism through inclusive support, resources, and community.

1 in 165

Girls aged 5–17 in Canada are diagnosed with autism, compared to 1 in 42 boys, highlighting underdiagnosis in females.

$1.2M - $4.7M

The estimated lifetime cost of supporting an individual with ASD in Canada, depending on severity and disability levels.

Higher Camouflaging in Females

Autistic women often mask their traits, leading to increased mental health struggles, making diagnosis and support more challenging.
